International Organization By Prof. Ameer Taimur

Prof. Ameer Taimur

Prof. Ameer Taimur is an accomplished Lecturer in Political Science with extensive experience spanning over seven years in higher education and academic administration. Currently serving in the Higher Education Department, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, he is engaged in teaching undergraduate courses and guiding students in academic excellence and research development.

He earned his Bachelor of Arts (Hons) degree from GC University Lahore, one of the leading institutions in Pakistan. His professional journey reflects a strong commitment to quality education, student mentorship, and the promotion of critical and analytical thinking in the field of political science.
