Introduction to Political Science

Political science examines how governments are organized, how decisions are made, and how power operates in societies. It blends institutional study with empirical insight into political behavior at all levels.

Evolution of the Field

  • Traditional: Focused on constitutions, institutions, and formal political structures.
  • Modern: Emphasizes behavior, public opinion, and evidence-based analysis.
