Introduction to Political Science

Political science is a comprehensive social science discipline dedicated to the systematic study of the state, government, politics, and power dynamics. It investigates the theory, description, analysis, and prediction of political behavior, systems, and institutions at local, state, national, and international levels.

Evolution of the Definition

  1. Traditional Approach (Legal/Institutional): Focused almost exclusively on the state, government, constitutions, and formal institutions, often with a normative approach to what "ought" to be.

  2. Modern Approach (Behavioural/Pluralistic): Emerged in the 20th century, focusing on political activities, human behavior, power, and informal processes, utilizing empirical, value-free, or "value-relevant" analysis.
